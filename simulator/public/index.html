How to operate the simulator

  1. Connect the local broker once. Wait for Ready to send.
  2. Choose a warehouse and bin, or select a group.
  3. Move the slider, choose a preset, or enter an exact value. The preview is a draft.
  4. Press Send reading. The simulator applies the new value and sends it once over MQTT.
  5. Open Sent readings & MQTT activity to inspect what was sent. With Ditto healthy and its native MQTT connection running, the matching bin's load feature is updated.

For a group, percentage mode respects each bin's capacity: 50% gives 5 kg, 25 kg and 50 kg for 10 kg, 50 kg and 100 kg bins. These are weight percentages, not volume measurements.
