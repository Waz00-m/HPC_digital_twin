# Warehouse overview

Mode: Operate. This is a new surface within the simulator's existing visual system: light neutral work surface, dark application header, semantic status colors and locally compiled daisyUI controls. The user approved bins grouped by warehouse with an inline detail panel. Preserve that inherited identity; no new branding, imagery, font or decorative motion is needed.

Organize the actual warehouse hierarchy into three sections, each containing its own bins. Each bin is a selectable measurement record: ID, textual status, current kg, rated load, normalized weight bar with a model-threshold tick, percentage and last receipt time. Clamp only the bar at full width; retain the actual percentage and overload text. Warehouse headings show summed received weight and summed rated capacity when all readings are available, or an explicit reading count when incomplete. Section membership does not imply physical rack positions.

Use warehouse filter buttons in the desktop sidebar and a compact warehouse select at widths up to 800 px; show only the appropriate warehouse control. Keep the status select alongside it on mobile. A selected bin opens an inline detail panel with the source reading, current model attributes, threshold, topic and ontology identity. Its selected outline and textual expanded state support the interaction; closing details returns focus to that bin. Preserve controls during automatic readback.

The page and header share a centered container capped at 1440 px, with 32 px desktop page insets. Warehouse sections show three measurement records across; below 560 px they stack with horizontal separators, 16 px page insets and a single-column detail list. The Scenario lab places a 290–360 px form beside flexible results, stacking at widths up to 800 px. Below 560 px these panels use 18 px internal padding. Keep the page free of horizontal overflow; labelled, keyboard-focusable result tables scroll within their own regions. The team schedule has its own scroll region capped at 400 px high.

Use the inherited system sans-serif stack and tabular numerals for measurements. Page titles scale from 1.65 to 2.1 rem; section titles are 1.125 rem. Supporting measurements, badges, receipts, table cells and chart axes remain readable at 14 px. Use white panels on the light neutral workspace, subtle dividers and restrained rounded corners: 12 px for the principal panels and smaller curves for filters and records. Primary color identifies primary actions, focus and selected bins. Success, warning and error retain their existing daisyUI meanings. Inputs and buttons keep a 44 px minimum height and a visible focus outline. Optional-field placeholders use the opaque muted text color rather than reduced opacity.

The Scenario lab shares the same header navigation, typography and panel treatment. Distinguish received readings from simulated outcomes with plain copy and the Snapshot simulation badge. Keep duration, delay and travel settings visible; group per-bin purposes/demand and burst/uncertainty settings in expandable sections. Each bin's settings identify its bin, warehouse and capacity, with Collection/Inventory purpose, nonnegative demand, alert percentage and service time. Compare strategies is the primary action; Save draft stores local configuration. Drafts contain assumptions rather than frozen telemetry or results, and legacy draft conversion is stated explicitly.

Results lead with the next job under least time remaining, dispatch/arrival/completion timing and a plain-language safety margin. Follow with delayed-start consequences, nominal strategy comparison, cumulative lost-kg chart, synthetic trials and the selected strategy's team schedule; keep per-bin outcomes and model assumptions expandable. Separate collection overflow from unmet inventory demand in the comparison table. The chart combines those losses for comparison, uses the existing neutral, secondary and primary colors with distinct line patterns, and supplies exact values in the table. Keep 14 px chart labels and the strategy legend readable. Changing Inspect strategy updates the schedule and per-bin outcomes; the next-job and delay sections remain explicitly tied to least time remaining.

Show snapshot time and bin count with the results. Disclose removed pre-existing snapshot excess there and in the assumptions. Explain that purpose, demand, travel and service are assumptions, trials are synthetic counts, and the rule is a heuristic; do not imply learned prediction or OWL reasoning. A settings change reveals the stale-result notice until the next successful comparison. Disable form controls during requests and announce progress/errors in the status region. Invalid fields inside expandable settings reveal their section. At widths up to 800 px a completed comparison scrolls to and focuses Handling decisions; View results provides the same return path from the form. Physical execution and live scheduling remain unconnected; no action changes live bins.

Use text as well as color for statuses. Normal, attention, overload, awaiting readings, absent configuration, invalid models and identity issues have distinct labels. On readback failure keep previously received data and explicitly label it last known; distinguish readback availability from native MQTT connection status. Read /api/fleet initially, on Refresh and every three seconds while visible. Percentage and status reflect the current imported model on each read, without requiring a new weight reading. A receipt timestamp does not establish that a bin is online. Never claim a broker acknowledgement means Ditto stored a value.
